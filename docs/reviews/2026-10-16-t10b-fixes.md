# T10b fixes review: proposed wording for SPEC.md design rule 2

Documented 2026-10-16 during T10b-fixes. Proposed wording to update SPEC.md design rule 2 (two-layer Fact-Checker) without changing interfaces.

## 1. Minute is always citable (layer 1)

In design rule 2, under the code check description:

"**Minute citation rule:** A number equal to `CandidateMoment.minute` that appears in a match-minute context (such as 'minute 24', 'at 37', '24th minute', '24. dakika', 'dakika 24') is verified without needing an explicit key in `evidence_refs`. Any other occurrence of that number (such as pass counts, metres, or durations) still requires a cited ledger key."

## 2. Possessive and locative opponent mentions are not actors (layer 1)

In design rule 2, under the attribution check description:

"**Opponent attribution rule:** In `check_actors`, the moment belongs to `labels['team']`. The opponent club and its players may only appear as the ones acted upon (e.g. after against, from, pressed, or as the direct object of verbs like leave, keep, push, pin, force, put under pressure) or when named as a place (followed by territory, half, box, area, goal, defence, defense, end, side, third, and Turkish equivalents such as yarı saha, ceza sahası, kale, savunma, bölge, or in possessives such as 'Ironvale's half' and 'Ironvale'in yarı sahası'). Active mentions of the opponent performing the moment's action remain rejected at layer 1."

## 3. Evaluation headline derivation

In evaluation reporting (`app/eval/report.py`):
The headline derives its verbs directly from the numbers: 'rose' or 'fell' only when the change in Groundedness is at least 0.1 on the 1 to 5 scale; otherwise it reports 'was unchanged (X vs Y)'. When planted error conditions run, the headline leads with the planted-error result and notes that in normal play both conditions scored similarly because first drafts were already accurate.
