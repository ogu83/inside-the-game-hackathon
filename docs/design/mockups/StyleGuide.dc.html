Radius 8 inner controls, 10 inputs and buttons, 14 cards and the pitch, pill for chips. Touch targets 44 px minimum, push-to-talk 56 px desktop, 64 px phone. Page gutter 24 desktop, 16 phone.

Pitch recipe

SVG pattern, 10 mowing stripes in pitch-a and pitch-b, white lines at 0.82 opacity, radial vignette to 0.45 black. Ball: white with a 1 px stroke in the possessing club's colour, soft glow, dotted trail of the last 6 touches in that colour. No raster texture needed.

Motion

Cue banner: slides up 16 px and fades in, 220 ms ease-out; holds for the cue duration; fades out 160 ms.
Ball: 250 ms linear tween between events, never a jump.
Push-to-talk: state change in under 50 ms; no animation that delays feedback.
Verdict chips: no motion; a rejected moment never flashes on the main view.
prefers-reduced-motion: fades only, ball steps without tween.
